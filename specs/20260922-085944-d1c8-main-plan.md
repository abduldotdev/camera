## Approach
We implement the right-edge vertical scrollbar and middle-scroll control isolation sequentially across UI, model, metadata, and test harness. In `CameraPopup.qml`, we attach a styled `ScrollBar.vertical` to `flick` matching the shell idiom, adjust `sectionsCol.width` for gutter clearance, and layer an overlay `MouseArea` (`acceptedButtons: Qt.NoButton`) over `sectionsCol` to intercept wheel events, scrolling `flick.contentY` while letting mouse clicks and drags pass directly to child controls. We eliminate the Digital Zoom wheel multiplier and its overlay, deleting obsolete constants from `Model.js` and `tests/model.test.js` to guarantee no slider reacts to wheel scrolling. We update `manifest.json` to 1.1.0 and align `README.md`. Finally, we extend the Quickshell smoke harness using `QtTest` `TestEvent` to verify slider invariance and scroll progression under synthetic wheel events.

## Risks
- **Click pass-through blockage**: Overlay interceptor could inadvertently block knob drag or clicks if buttons are accepted. *Detection & Mitigation*: Step 1 sets `acceptedButtons: Qt.NoButton`; verified in Step 4 by testing that underlying controls respond to clicks while wheel is intercepted.
- **Scrollbar clipping**: Narrow width could cause slider knobs to clip against the scrollbar. *Detection & Mitigation*: Step 1 reserves a 12px gutter on `sectionsCol`; verified visually and via layout inspection.
- **Harness slider coordinate mismatch**: Synthetic events could miss targets if popup positioning shifts. *Detection & Mitigation*: Step 4 uses `mapToItem` to map target slider coordinates dynamically in `shell.qml`.
